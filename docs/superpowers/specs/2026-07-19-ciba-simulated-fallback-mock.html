CIBA Simulated Fallback

Five states of CIBAPanel.js's "Try It" tab and the transfer step-up bridge, when PingOne's /as/bc-authorize fails and ciba_failover_mode=fallback_simulated takes over. The panel on top of each frame is what the user actually sees — pixel-identical to today. The card underneath is what's true internally at that moment and does not render anywhere in the app.
