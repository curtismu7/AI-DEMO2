Historical: this is the mock the design was approved from. Two things changed in review before shipping. The per-request accent no longer recolours the spinner — REGRESSION_PLAN.md H3 bans inline colour, so the accent is now a stylesheet token and the per-request colour reads on the card's top border instead; the swatches in section 01 and the palette strip in section 03 are kept as a record of the option that was considered. And reduced motion now stops every animation rather than slowing it. NeuralSpinner.jsx is the source of truth.

NeuralSpinner

Eight coloured tokens fall down their synapses into a pulsing core while a full-spectrum comet sweeps the rim. Pure CSS, no new dependencies, one file. Everything below is driven by two custom properties — --ns-size and --ns-accent — so the random per-request colour spinnerService already picks keeps working.

02

Scales down cleanly

every dimension is a ratio of --ns-size

Nothing is hardcoded in px except the ring hairlines, so the same component covers the overlay, a button, and a table cell. Below 40px the tokens stop being legible — that's the floor, and InlineSpinner still owns anything smaller.
